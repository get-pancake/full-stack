Failure modes

Why towers fall.

Most attempts die on construction, not taste. Three ways it goes wrong, and what we do about each.

  1. 01

    It squashes.

    Fluffy pancakes compress under 45 in of stack, unevenly. The tower sinks and tilts while you build.

    FixThin batter, no leavening. About 0.19 in cooked.

  2. 02

    It leans.

    Each pancake sits a hair off. 270 hairs add up to a lean you can’t fix at the top.

    Fix¼ turn per pancake. Two lasers. Drift called every 10.

  3. 03

    It gets greedy.

    The record is in reach, so you add five more. The fifth one is the one that falls.

    FixSlow at 44 in. Stop at 45 in. Hands off.

Recipe

A pancake built to carry weight.

Still a real pancake: edible, cooked on both sides, served after the attempt. One batch is 250 g of flour.

Ratios by weight.

Flour = 100.

Method.

  1. Weigh, whisk, rest 20 min.One batch at a time, so every batch is the same.
  2. Griddle at 330–350 °F, checked.A surface thermometer, not the dial. Heat is what thickens the batter.
  3. Ring down, one dose in.1.02 cup for 9 in, 0.44 cup for 7 in. Don’t spread, don’t press.
  4. Flip once, when the top goes matte.Pale golden, not brown.
  5. Cool flat on a rack, 18 min.Never stack warm. Trapped steam turns into a slippery film.
  6. Stack 10, measure.About 1.86 in. Re-plan the count from what you measure.

Doses, drawn to scale.

Same ring, same cup, every time. The 9 in pancakes come out a little thicker.

Build protocol

The tower, to scale.

Nothing touches it but pancakes. Lasers read plumb. A freestanding gauge reads height.

The stack

  • 145 pancakes at 9 in, the base
  • 125 pancakes at 7 in, the top
  • Slow at 44 in. Stop at 45 in.
  • Hold 5 s, hands off. Then measure.

The moves

  • Nothing touches the tower but pancakes.
  • Lower each one from an edge. Never slide.
  • A ¼ turn per pancake.
  • Spotters call drift every 10 pancakes.

  • Stacker.

    Places every pancake. Same person at every rehearsal.

  • Passer.

    Hands each one over on a thin spatula, at stack height.

  • Spotters ×2.

    One per laser. They call the drift every 10 pancakes.

  • Zone guard.

    Keeps it silent. No traffic, nobody near the table.

Rules

Inside the rules, with margin.

  • Each pancake 4.92 to 9.84 in across, measured before stacking. We use 7 and 9 in.
  • 0.39 in thick at most. Ours are about 0.19 in.
  • Freestanding. No support inside or out, nothing holding layers together. Standard ingredients only.
  • Stands 5 seconds after the last pancake.
  • Edible, and eaten or donated. Nothing in the trash.
